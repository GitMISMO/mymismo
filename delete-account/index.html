Delete Your Data

How to delete a MyMISMO account and the data that goes with it.

In the App

Summit attendee account: open MyMISMO, tap the gear (Settings), then Delete My Attendee Account and tap again to confirm. It happens right away.

MISMO Trivia account (once ranked play opens): Settings, then Delete My Trivia Account.

Everything saved on your phone (your schedule, settings and greeting name): Settings, then Clear Saved Data.

By Email

If you can't use the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and its data within 30 days.

Email a Deletion Request

What Gets Deleted

  • Attendee account: your profile, headshot and contact details, every conversation you were part of, and blocks that name you. You're also taken out of the attendee directory.
  • Trivia account: your player name, every leaderboard score and championship result, and your question history.
  • Your phone: your schedule, settings and greeting name.

What We Keep

Reports other attendees made about conduct are kept so the MISMO team can follow up. Your summit registration itself is part of MBA's event registration and isn't changed by deleting your MyMISMO account. Contact MBA to change your registration.
