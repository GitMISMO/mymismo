Privacy Policy

What MyMISMO collects, why, and how to delete it.

Who We Are

MyMISMO is the app of the Mortgage Industry Standards Maintenance Organization (MISMO), a subsidiary of the Mortgage Bankers Association. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to delete your information. Write to info@mismo.org with any question.

The Short Version

  • You can use most of MyMISMO without signing in. The agenda, speakers, sponsors, webinars and everything else that's public on mismo.org stays public in the app.
  • MyMISMO has no advertising, sells nothing about you, and doesn't track what you do in the app.
  • Your schedule, settings and name for the greeting stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in, we keep only what that feature needs, and you can delete it in the app at any time.

What Stays on Your Phone

Your starred sessions (My Schedule), the tiles you pick for your dashboard, dark mode, the first name you type for your greeting, and practice trivia scores are saved on your phone only. MISMO never receives them. Settings, Clear Saved Data removes all of them.

What We Don't Do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or renting of your information.
  • No location tracking, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics that follow what you do in the app.
  • We don't use your information to make automated decisions about you.

Emails

The only email MyMISMO sends is the sign-in code you ask for. MyMISMO doesn't add you to any mailing list.

Who Helps Us Run MyMISMO

These companies process information only to run the app for MISMO:

  • GitHub hosts the app and stores the attendee, message and trivia records in private storage.
  • Amazon Web Services runs the small server that checks sign-ins and saves messages. It looks at your network address briefly to stop abuse and doesn't store it.
  • Microsoft sends the sign-in code email.
  • Apple and Google deliver alerts if you turn them on.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typeface, and pictures and logos load from the MISMO website. Videos play from YouTube, under YouTube's own privacy policy, only when you press play.

Links to mismo.org, registration pages and other sites open in your browser, and those sites' own policies apply there.

How Long We Keep It

We keep attendee profiles, messages, reports and trivia records until you delete them or ask us to. Sign-in codes are cleared within a day. Alert addresses are removed when you turn alerts off or your phone's notification service says the address no longer works.

Deleting Your Information

In the app: Settings, Delete My Attendee Account removes your attendee profile, your messages and blocks that name you. Once MISMO Trivia ranked play opens, players can delete their trivia account in the same place. You can also ask us by email: see Delete Your Data. Settings, Clear Saved Data removes everything kept on your phone.

Children

MyMISMO is made for mortgage industry professionals and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information MyMISMO keeps about you by writing to info@mismo.org. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date below.
