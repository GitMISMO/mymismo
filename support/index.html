Help and Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach MISMO.

Getting Started

MyMISMO is free and works without an account. Open it to see MISMO news, webinars, certifications and, during a summit, the full agenda, speakers, sponsors and the hotel guide.

Common Questions

How do I build my schedule?
Tap the star on any session. My Schedule lists them, and Add to Calendar puts a session in your phone's calendar with a reminder.

How do I message other attendees?
In Settings, sign in as a summit attendee with the email you registered with. We'll email you a six-digit code. Turn on Listed in your profile so others can find you.

Someone is bothering me. What can I do?
Open their profile and tap Block, so they can't message you, or Report to tell the MISMO team. In a conversation, tap Profile, Block or Report at the top.

How do I get alerts?
Settings, Summit Alerts. We only send room changes and news from the MISMO team during a summit.

How do I delete my account?
Settings, Delete My Attendee Account. More on Delete Your Data.

Dark mode?
Settings, Appearance.

Contact Us
