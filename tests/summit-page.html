WHY ATTEND

The Next Chapter Starts in New Orleans

New Orleans. New Ideas. Lasting Change.

The mortgage industry is changing fast, and the standards and technology that hold it together are changing as well. This January, MISMO brings together lenders, servicers, regulators, GSEs, government agencies, service providers and technology providers at the InterContinental New Orleans for four days of collaboration that turns ideas into industry-wide progress.

Plan the Summit your way. On January 12, hear from industry leaders in a full day of sessions and panels. On January 11, 13 and 14, roll up your sleeves in MISMO workgroups and workshops, where the standards that power the mortgage industry’s digital future take shape.

Whether you help develop the standards or are just starting to explore them, there’s a seat for you at the table. Come for the conversations, stay for the connections, and leave with ideas you can put to work.

Will you join us to create lasting change?
